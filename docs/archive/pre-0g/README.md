# Archive — Casper Agentic Buildathon 2026

**These documents are historical. They describe AgentGate as it was submitted to the
Casper Agentic Buildathon 2026 Final Round (deadline 2026-07-26), when the project ran
on Casper Testnet.**

AgentGate has since migrated to **0G Galileo Testnet** — Solidity contracts, native OG,
payments bound to their invoice by a `PaymentRouter` contract, and no indexer anywhere in
the read path. Nothing in this directory describes the current system, and the commands,
contract hashes, transaction hashes, prices and env vars in it no longer work.

They are kept, unedited, because they are a record of what was actually submitted and
judged. Rewriting them to say "0G" would make them a false record rather than an accurate
old one.

| `VIDEO_SCRIPT.md` | Scene-by-scene demo video script |

| `PRD-CONFORMANCE.md` | Point-in-time audit (2026-06-12) against the PRD |
